How often should you follow up with a lead?

Short answer: four times over a week, and stop the moment they reply.

The first reply catches the buyer while they are still looking. The next three touches each bring something new: a quick check-in, a useful resource and a polite last call.

Myth

Following up more than once looks desperate.

Reality

Buyers get busy. A useful nudge is a service, not pressure.

Dijae's rule

Follow up until they decide, not until they get annoyed.

What can you set up today?

  1. Write the four messages in one document.
  2. Add a stop rule: any reply pauses the sequence.
  3. Name the person who takes over a reply.
