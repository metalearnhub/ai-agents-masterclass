1. Know which tool does what

The most common confusion is between the two AI features. They do different jobs:

ToolJob in the system
BotcakeThe automation layer: flows, keywords, default replies, comment replies, sequences, rules and broadcasts.
Botcake AIReplies to customers by itself using an AI agent you configure.
PancakeThe team inbox: all channels in one place, tags, conversation assignment, quick replies, POS orders and CRM.
Pancake AI AssistantOnly suggests replies to your agents. A person still clicks Send.

Pancake AI Assistant does not reply to customers on its own. If you want AI to reply automatically, use Botcake AI. Both now draw from the same Pancake prepaid wallet. For reference, check the documentation here.

2. Connect Pancake and Botcake

Do this before you touch the AI. If the two apps are not synced, the bot and your team will work blind to each other.

1Sync tags both ways (in Botcake). Go to Botcake › Settings › Tag and turn on both options:

  • Sync Tag from Pancake to Botcake in real-time
  • Sync Pancake Tag from Botcake to Pancake in real-time

Create your tags in Pancake first, then use them in Botcake. In the same screen you can attach or remove a sequence when a tag is added, which is how tag-based follow-up works. For reference, check the documentation here.

2Turn on ecosystem sync (in Pancake). Go to Pancake › Settings › General › Ecosystem Synchronization:

  • Botcake Notifications: shows a [Botcake Reply] prefix on messages sent by the bot, so agents know what the customer has already been told.
  • Real-time Botcake Tag Synchronization: keeps tags identical in both apps.

3Decide what happens when the bot fails or a person replies (in Botcake). In Botcake › Settings › General, under Conversation Status on Pancake When the Bot Responds, choose how failed bot replies are marked in Pancake and how long the bot pauses while a staff member is actively replying, so the bot never talks over your agent. For reference, check the documentation here.

Check this if you use Meta's Business AIMeta only lets one app reply in a conversation at a time. If your Page also uses Meta Business Agent, open your Page's Settings › Advanced Messaging, click Edit on Botcake and on Pancake v2, and enable the take-control permission for both before passing any conversation to Meta's agent. Skip this and Botcake and Pancake cannot take the conversation back.

3. Fund the AI wallet and switch on Botcake AI

Botcake AI is now pay-as-you-go. Every reply uses tokens, and the tokens are paid from a Pancake prepaid account in USD. One prepaid account covers WhatsApp Business messaging, Pancake AI Assistant and Botcake AI Assistant, so you only fund one wallet.

1Open the prepaid accounts. In Botcake, click your avatar at the top right, open Profile › Prepaid account and click + Add prepaid account. This opens Pancake's wallet page at account.pancake.vn.

2Create the account. Click + Create account, enter an Account name (for example "Botcake") and click Create account. The card confirms what it pays for: WhatsApp Business messages, Pancake AI assistant and Botcake AI assistant, in USD only.

3Top up. Choose a Top-up amount (the options start at $10), add your card under Card information, check the total on the right and click Next to pay. Your balance appears in the wallet list, and every account row has its own Top up button for later.

4Point Botcake at the wallet. Back in Botcake, open Settings › Account › Account Balance. Confirm the balance, then under Account Information pick your wallet in the Prepaid account dropdown and set Auto top-up amount and Auto top-up when balance is below. Management takes you back to the Pancake wallet.

5Check the master switch. Open Settings › System › Integrations. The AI row should say Connected. Click the pencil icon to open Configure AI integration, where the Botcake AI model has a toggle. That toggle turns AI on or off for the whole account. There is no API key to paste: Botcake manages the model for you.

Dijae's ruleTurn on auto top-up before you go live. An AI agent that stops in the middle of a sales conversation because the wallet hit zero costs you more than the tokens ever will. Set the trigger amount high enough to cover a busy weekend.

4. Set up the Botcake AI page

Open Advance › Botcake AI in the left menu. The page header reads "Each Agent can be used in multiple features," and the Category list on the left has five parts:

CategoryWhat it holds
Agent listYour default agent and all agents, plus + Create new agent.
InformationBusiness details shared by every agent: shop name, phone, email, address, countries and payment details.
Knowledge sourceThe shared library of files, website links and images your agents can rely on.
StatisticsUsage reports.
GeneralBalance, default agent, reply timing, permissions, speech-to-text and chat history.

1Fill in Information once. Enter your business details and click Update. Every agent can attach this block, so you do not repeat your address and payment methods in each instruction.

2Load the Knowledge source library. It has two views, File instruction for documents and Attachment for images the agent can send. Use Upload for files or Config to pick files from Google Drive.

Upload opens Import data. You can drop in Docx, PDF, TXT, XLSX or MD files up to 15 MB, or enter a website domain and click Check URL so the AI searches that site live when it answers. Live web search only works with Botcake AI models, and sites with pop-ups or anti-robot checks may block it.

3Review General. Check the Account Balance, choose the Select Default Agent, and set Smart delay (how long the AI waits after a customer message) and Maximum length of a message. The same page has Permissions & Security, speech-to-text and AI sync image search for POS products.

Further down, Enable chat history lets the AI remember the last 50 messages of a conversation, and Clear chat wipes it. Keep history on: an agent that forgets what the customer said three messages ago asks the same questions twice.

6. Write and optimize the instructions

This is the part that decides whether your AI sells or embarrasses you. The Instruction tab holds a Business information card (your Information block, optional) and the instruction editor, which now takes up to 15,000 characters. Type { to insert a customer field such as the first name, and @ to insert a picture.

Not sure where to start? Click Reference template under the editor. Botcake offers six example instructions you can preview and copy.

The 9-part instruction structure

A strong agent instruction follows the same order. Each part answers one question the AI would otherwise guess at:

# 1. ROLE
Who the AI is, for which business, on which channel.

# 2. GOAL
The one outcome every conversation should move toward
(e.g. a confirmed order, a booked appointment).

# 3. BUSINESS FACTS
Name, location, hours, delivery areas, payment methods.

# 4. PRODUCTS AND PRICES
Only what the AI may quote. Point to the knowledge file
for the full catalog.

# 5. POLICIES
Shipping, returns, warranty, promos. Exact wording.

# 6. TONE AND LANGUAGE
Formality, length, emoji use, language rules.

# 7. CONVERSATION STEPS
The questions to ask, in order, before recommending
or taking an order.

# 8. RULES (DO / DON'T)
What the AI must never say, promise or invent.

# 9. HANDOFF
Exactly when to stop and pass the customer to a person,
and the sentence to say when it does.

A filled-in example

Here is the structure written out for a fictional skincare shop in Manila. It is the same instruction used in the screenshots below. Replace every detail with your own:

# ROLE
You are Bea, the online sales assistant of Glow Lab PH,
a skincare shop in Quezon City. You chat with customers
on Facebook Messenger and Instagram.

# GOAL
Help each customer choose the right product and confirm
an order. A complete order needs: product, quantity,
full name, phone number and delivery address.

# BUSINESS FACTS
- Store hours: Mon-Sat, 9 AM-6 PM. You reply 24/7.
- Delivery: nationwide via J&T. Metro Manila 1-3 days,
  provinces 3-7 days.
- Payment: GCash, bank transfer, or COD (Metro Manila only).

# PRODUCTS AND PRICES
Quote prices only from the attached file "Glow Lab
Products". If a product is not in the file, say you
will check with the team. Never estimate a price.

# POLICIES
- Returns: unopened items within 7 days of delivery.
- Promo: free shipping on orders of PHP 1,500 and up.
  Do not offer any other discount.

# TONE AND LANGUAGE
- Friendly, polite and respectful. Address customers
  as Sir or Ma'am unless they give their name.
- Reply in the language the customer uses.
- Maximum 3 short sentences per message. 1 emoji max.

# CONVERSATION STEPS
1. Greet the customer by first name: {{user.first_name}}.
2. Ask their skin type and main concern.
3. Recommend 1 product (2 at most) and explain why
   in one sentence.
4. If they want to buy, collect the order details one
   question at a time.
5. Repeat the full order back and ask them to confirm.

# RULES
- Never give medical advice. For allergies, rashes or
  pregnancy questions, hand off to a person.
- Never promise delivery dates outside the ranges above.
- Never say you are human. If asked, say you are Glow
  Lab's AI assistant and a person can join anytime.

# HANDOFF
Hand off when the customer: is angry or complaining,
asks for a person, asks about a wholesale or reseller
deal, or asks something not covered here.
Say: "One moment, please. I'm passing you to our team
so they can help you directly."
Weak instructionWhy it failsStronger version
"Be helpful and sell our products."No goal, no limits. The AI improvises prices and promises.Name the goal (a confirmed order) and the exact details an order needs.
"Answer all questions."It will answer medical, legal and pricing questions it should not.List what it must never answer and when to hand off.
"Use a friendly tone."Too vague. Replies become long and generic.Set length, language and emoji rules, as in the example.

Let Botcake optimize it, then review the changes

Click AI enhancements above the editor. The Optimize instructions window shows Your instructions on the left and Instructions after optimization on the right. Use the refresh icon to re-optimize, the copy icon to keep a version, and Apply only when you agree with the result. In our test, the optimizer reorganized the example into Role and Task sections and added the customer's first name field.

Dijae's ruleTreat an optimization like a code change: read the difference before you apply it. Check that every price, policy and handoff rule survived. Optimizers improve structure, but they can soften a "never" into a "try not to," and that one word is where AI agents start promising things your business cannot deliver. Write the handoff section first and confirm it is still there after every optimization.

7. Test, check the cost and train answers

1Test in Preview. Type a real customer message in the Preview chat. In our test, "Hi Bea! what can you do" returned a greeting by first name, a one-line summary of what Bea can do, and the first question from the conversation steps.

2Check what each reply costs. Hover the $ icon under a reply to see Message cost: input tokens, output tokens and the estimated cost. Our first reply used 3,136 input tokens and 58 output tokens, for an estimated $0.0013. Input tokens are mostly your instructions, which are sent with every reply, so a long instruction costs a little on every message.

3Train the answers you care about. Click the wand icon, Improve AI response, under any reply. In Improved AI responses, write the Customer question and the exact AI response you want, then click Save. Saved examples appear in a list, and Add new adds more. Refresh the Preview and ask again to confirm the agent uses your trained answer.

Use trained answers for the moments that matter most: the first greeting, price questions, delivery questions and the handoff sentence. Fix general behavior in the instructions; fix specific answers here.

8. Add knowledge files

Long product lists, policies and FAQs belong in files, not in the instruction box. Open the agent's Knowledge tab and click Add files. The Add files knowledge window has two tabs: File instructions (files from your Knowledge source library, or Upload a new one) and Link URL. Tick the files and click Add to instruction.

Uploaded files are stored in the knowledge base, while for URL sources the AI searches the website each time it answers, so that information stays current. Use files for anything that must be exact (prices, policies) and links for content that changes often.

Botcake's file search reads documents in small chunks, so how you format a file matters. Follow five rules:

  1. One topic per section, with a clear heading.
  2. Keyword-rich headings, such as "Return Policy" or "Product: Glow Serum".
  3. Short sections of 1 to 2 paragraphs.
  4. Bullets instead of tables.
  5. Repeat the product name in each section instead of writing "this product".
Product: Glow Lab Vitamin C Serum (30 ml)
- Price: PHP 650
- Best for: dull skin, dark spots
- How to use the Glow Lab Vitamin C Serum: 3-4 drops
  every morning before sunscreen.
- In stock: yes

Return Policy - Glow Lab
- Unopened items can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- Opened items cannot be returned unless damaged on arrival.

9. Connect Pancake POS for auto-orders

Open the agent's Tools tab. Under Integrate connection, the built-in options are Pancake POS ("Automation create order") and Pancake CRM ("CRM Lead Automation"). Custom tools go under Tools with Add new.

Click Pancake POS to open Automation create order:

OptionWhat it does
Auto-create order when information is availableCreates the Pancake POS order once the AI has collected the order details.
Automatic Upsell/Cross-sell based on POS configurationLets the AI suggest add-ons using your POS setup.
Stop auto-creating orders when receiving negative reports on PancakeSkips automatic orders for customers with negative reports.
Image sending methodSend as single image or Send as image collection.
Import productImport all POS products, or none, so the AI quotes real names, prices and photos.

An honest limitation: the AI can only sell what it can see. In our test account no POS products were imported, so when asked "what are your products," the agent correctly said it had no product list and asked about the customer's skin instead. That is the behavior you want, but it also means an empty catalog produces a polite agent that cannot close. For reference, check the documentation here.

10. Follow-ups and the human handoff

Open the agent's AI response behavior tab. It has two parts, switched at the top right: Auto follow-up and Special case.

Auto follow-up

Turn on Auto follow-up to message customers who go quiet. Set Send follow-up messages automatically after (30 minutes in our test), the Number of messages (3), and whether follow-ups are written Automatically with AI or Manually. One or two follow-ups is usually enough. Remember that Meta limits what you can send more than 24 hours after a customer's last message.

Special case: assign a person and pause the AI

This is the handoff. Turn on Special case ("AI automatically detects & handles special situations in the conversation as configured") and click Add case.

1Choose a template in Select template: When the order is created successfully, When the order creation fails or When the customer sends a phone number.

2Click Add action. Bot Action offers Add tag, Remove tag, Assign to Staff on Pancake, Start a Flow, Set User Field and Notify Admins. AI action offers Pause AI.

3Choose Assign to Staff on Pancake. Pick the staff in Select staff and set the rules: Assign randomizer, Replace staff (replace an existing assignment), Only assign to online staff, or Only assign to available staff on Pancake (staff whose receive mode is Available). Click Save.

4Add a second action, Pause AI. Choose Pause AI for a period of time (for example 1 hour; the AI resumes automatically) or Pause AI until turned back on (the AI only replies again when a staff member reactivates it). Then click Save at the top of the workspace.

The result is a clean human-in-the-loop: the AI handles the conversation and creates the order, a person in Pancake confirms it, and the AI stays quiet while they do. Pair it with a tag your Pancake team filters on, so no handed-off customer waits unseen. You can still place the AI inside a Botcake Flow with an AI block when you need different steps for successful replies, failed replies and special intents. For reference, check the documentation here.

11. Go live and test

  1. Set the default agent. In General or the Agent list, make sure your agent is the default.
  2. Choose who replies first. At the top of the Botcake AI page, the Default reply priority / AI priority switch decides whether your default reply or the AI answers.
  3. Confirm the wallet. Balance above zero, auto top-up on, and the Botcake AI toggle in Integrations switched on.
  4. Watch the first week. Read real conversations every day, check message costs, and fix the instructions or train answers where the AI hesitated or guessed.

Define "ready to go live" before you test

Treat the agent like any product release: agree on acceptance criteria first, then test against them. These are the minimum checks I recommend:

CheckPasses when
Prices and policiesEvery price, fee and policy the AI quotes matches your file exactly, across at least 20 test questions.
Unknown questionsWhen the answer is not in its instructions or files, the AI says it will check with the team instead of guessing.
HandoffComplaints, requests for a person and created orders all reach a staff member in Pancake, and the AI pauses.
OrdersA test order with complete details appears in Pancake POS. An order with missing details does not.
CostThe average message cost in Preview fits your budget at your expected monthly conversation volume.
Tone and lengthReplies follow your language, length and emoji rules on mobile.

After launch, track a few numbers weekly: share of conversations handed off, orders created by the AI, wallet spend, and the questions it could not answer. Those tell you where to improve next. For a full test method, see How to Test an AI Chatbot Before It Talks to Your Customers.

12. Let Pancake AI Assistant help your team

Once a customer reaches a person, Pancake AI Assistant speeds up the human side. Set it up in Pancake › Settings › AI Assistant:

  • AI reply suggestions: 3 suggested replies under the message box. Press Tab to cycle and Enter to send.
  • Sentiment detection: automatically tags angry or frustrated customers (for example "Needs manager") so a manager can step in.
  • Dataset: train it on 20 to 200 of your best past conversations plus 1 or 2 policy files.

It is billed per token from the same USD prepaid wallet. The Suggest frequently bought products feature is free. For reference, check the documentation here.

Dijae's ruleAssistive AI before full automation. Let the AI suggest while your team stays in control, especially for closing sales and handling complaints, where customers need a real person. Your team learns from what the AI suggests, you see where it is reliable, and you automate the parts that have proven themselves.

13. Common mistakes

  • No auto top-up. The wallet runs dry on a busy day and the AI goes silent mid-conversation.
  • Applying an optimization without reading it. Rules and handoff lines can change. Compare before you click Apply.
  • Prices in the instruction box and in a file. When the two disagree, the AI may quote the wrong one. Keep prices in one place.
  • An empty POS catalog with auto-orders on. The AI cannot create orders for products it cannot see.
  • No handoff case. Every AI needs an exit to a person, and someone in Pancake must be watching for it.
  • Tags synced in one direction only. Your team sees the bot's tags, but the bot never sees your team's (or the other way around).
  • Handover Protocol left on. If you only use Botcake and Pancake, do not use Handover Protocol. A wrong setup breaks keywords, buttons, inputs, sequences and rules. For reference, check the documentation here.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own OpenAI API key for Botcake AI?

No. Botcake AI now runs on Botcake-managed models (OpenAI GPT and DeepSeek) and is paid per token from a Pancake prepaid account in USD. You only fund the wallet and keep the Botcake AI toggle on in Settings › Integrations.

How much does a Botcake AI reply cost?

It depends on the model and the length of your instructions. Botcake shows the input tokens, output tokens and estimated cost under every Preview reply. In our test on GPT-4.1 mini, one reply cost an estimated $0.0013.

Does Pancake AI Assistant reply to customers automatically?

No. It only suggests replies, and your agent clicks Send. To have AI reply to customers automatically, use Botcake AI.

Can Botcake AI create orders in Pancake POS?

Yes. In the agent's Tools tab, open Pancake POS and turn on Auto-create order when information is available. Import your products first so the AI can quote and order them.

How do I hand a customer to my team after an order?

In the agent's AI response behavior tab, turn on Special case, add a case with the template When the order is created successfully, then add the actions Assign to Staff on Pancake and Pause AI.
