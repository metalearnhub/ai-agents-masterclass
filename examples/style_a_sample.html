Most leads are not lost on the first message. They are lost when nobody follows up. This guide shows the follow-up system step by step: when to reply, what to send, when to stop and when a person takes over.

What you will get

  • A four-touch follow-up sequence you can copy.
  • The stop rules that keep it polite.
  • A handoff trigger for real buyers.

1. The follow-up sequence

A good sequence is short, specific and stops the moment the buyer replies.

Dijae's rule

Follow up until they decide, not until they get annoyed. Every message should give the buyer something new.
