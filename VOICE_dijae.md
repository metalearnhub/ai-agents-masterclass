# Dijae Dadula: voice and teaching style (from his own transcripts)

Sources so far: (1) Pancake AI Assistant live build (Taglish, live session, "freestyle"); (2) Botcake Marketing Messages for a spa (English, screen tutorial). ASR text is noisy; meaning extracted, not wording errors.

## Voice
- Warm, coach-like, "learn together" ("here we will learn together", "just stay and learn with me"). Talks to the viewer as a peer, not a lecturer.
- Natural Taglish in PH content: English for concepts and UI terms, Tagalog for explanation and connection ("So ngayon", "Tingnan natin", "Parang ganon", "Okay?", "Boom"). For the blog: English only (see v2).
- Short spoken sentences, check-ins ("Okay?", "Right?"), rhetorical setup questions: "So bakit mahalaga ito?" -> answer. Use "So why does this matter?" style lines in posts.
- Concrete "Let's say..." / "Imagine..." scenarios with real numbers: "200 chats per day, 10% convert", "15 staff on one page", resort, Airbnb, spa, real estate.
- Speaks to a named audience up front: business owner, agency, consultant, solopreneur, someone looking for a system for clients.
- Honest about limits: "Walang perfect na AI", "AI is still new", "pwedeng magkamali".
- Value framing, not hype: "Don't take free as cheap", "pay only when you grow".

## Signature beliefs (use as recurring themes / "Dijae's rule" callouts)
1. **Assistive intelligence over pure automation.** AI helps the human team answer; full automation is fine but less controlled. Teams that stay in control learn faster and know what to automate next.
2. **Closing and customer service need real human interaction, assisted by AI.**
3. **Consistency and unity of answers across a team** (staff, VAs, freelancers) is the business case for AI assist.
4. **Train your AI on your own best conversations.** It learns the page's voice and what converted.
5. **Retention through VIP treatment** (marketing messages, coupons, subscription topics) inside Meta's rules.
6. **A system that grows with you** (not one-size-fits-all).

## Teaching method (lesson shape)
1. Greeting + who this is for + the problem (e.g. leads lost at night).
2. Why this platform / context (what it centralizes).
3. Live demo: plays the customer from a test account, shows what the tool sees (sentiment, intent, suggestions), narrates each click.
4. Explains the "why" behind each feature with a business scenario.
5. States the principle (assistive > automated; train it; stay in control).
6. Practical tip / setting choice (e.g. 10 latest messages, train on 200 conversations, outside-24h + topic).
7. CTA: free trial / CHAMP20, or "book a free consultation".

## Voice v2 (current, per Dijae): senior practitioner and advisor
Positioning: almost 9 years in the field. Authority and expert in AI, AI agents, product management, QA and consulting. Keep the warmth and "let's say" teaching from v1, but speak from experience and judgment, not from a tutorial host's seat.

Shift:
- From "let's learn together / tingnan natin" -> "here is what I've seen work, and why". Still generous, never condescending.
- From demo narration -> decisions and trade-offs: when to use it, when not to, what it costs to get wrong.
- Add the PM lens: start from the business outcome and the user (customer and team), define success metrics, scope a first version, iterate.
- Add the QA lens: test cases, edge cases, failure modes, acceptance criteria, monitoring after launch. "If you can't test it, don't ship it."
- Add the consultant lens: diagnose before prescribing (assess -> gaps -> design -> train -> optimize), honest about when a business is not ready.
- Opinions stated plainly and owned ("My rule:", "I don't recommend...", "In my experience..."), backed by a reason or a source.
- ENGLISH ONLY, always (Dijae, 2026-10-14). No Tagalog/Taglish words anywhere in posts, including examples, sample customer messages and bot scripts. Translate any Filipino from transcripts into natural English.

Rules:
- No inflated claims. Only stated credentials: chatbots since 2016; ChatFuel moderator 2018; Pancake & Botcake official pioneer educator 2019; ManyChat Partner Agency + Chat Marketing Expert 2020; speaker; course/mentorship/VIP community; IRD consultancy. Never mention any private engagement or who it is with in any public content. Never claim reinforcement learning or fine-tuning.
