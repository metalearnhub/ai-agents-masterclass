Replying to every message is not the hard part anymore. Most chat tools can do that. The hard part is knowing which conversations are real buyers, and getting those people to the right person fast.

Imagine a shop with a busy Facebook Page. Hundreds of messages a day: prices, stock, delivery, "is this still available?" The team answers as many as they can, in the order they arrive. The serious buyer who asked at 2 p.m. waits behind forty people asking the same price question, and buys somewhere else.
