AI assistants recommend a business by searching the web, reading a small set of pages and citing the ones that answer the question clearly. None of the big platforms sells a spot in those answers, and none publishes a ranking formula. What they do publish is how their crawlers work and what makes a page eligible, and that is enough to know where a business can lose.

Picture this · illustrative

A clinic owner in Cavite asks ChatGPT for the best dental clinics nearby that open on Sundays. The answer names three clinics and links two websites. Her clinic is open on Sundays and has good reviews, but it is not in the answer. Her website never says "open Sundays" in plain text, and its booking page is a picture of a schedule.

An AI recommendation is an answer the assistant writes after reading pages it can reach, understand and trust enough to cite.

What happens between the question and the answer?

Short answer: the assistant decides whether to search, runs one or more searches, reads the pages it finds, and writes an answer that cites some of them.

Each step is a filter. OpenAI says ChatGPT decides whether to search based on what the user asks, and that it also uses the context of the conversation for follow-up questions (OpenAI, October 31, 2024). Google says its AI Overviews and AI Mode may use "query fan-out": several related searches across subtopics, which surface a wider set of supporting links than a classic results page (Google Search Central).

So one customer question can become several searches. A page only gets a chance if a crawler could fetch it, the search index holds it, and its text matches one of those searches well enough to be read. Only then does the model decide what to quote and link.

What does each assistant say it needs?

Short answer: a page its search crawler may fetch, that is indexed, and that answers clearly. None of them asks for special AI files or markup.

Notice what is missing from every one of these pages: a list of ranking factors. OpenAI's launch post describes how ChatGPT decides to search and where sources appear, but not how it chooses between two clinics. Treat anyone who claims to know the exact formula with care.

Can a business pay to be recommended?

Short answer: not in the organic answer. The platforms describe how to be eligible, not how to buy placement.

OpenAI says any website or publisher can choose to appear in ChatGPT search (OpenAI). It also lists content partners such as the Associated Press and Reuters, which supply news directly. That is a content deal for news publishers, not a paid slot for local businesses.

Ads are a separate product with their own labels. If a vendor promises your business will be "recommended by ChatGPT" for a fee, ask what exactly they control. The honest answer is your pages, your listings and your reputation, not the model.

Myth

You need a special AI file or new schema to show up in AI answers.

Reality

Google says no new AI text files or special schema are required. Crawl access, indexing and clear answers come first.

Where do most businesses drop out?

Short answer: before the model ever reads them: blocked crawlers, pages that are not indexed, and key facts that are not written as text.

Go back to the clinic in the opening scene. It was not rejected for quality. It was never in the pool, because the fact the customer asked about was inside an image. The same happens when opening hours live only in a Facebook post, prices sit in a PDF, or a security plugin blocks every bot by default.

Writing helps too. A 2024 study presented at the ACM KDD conference found that adding cited sources, quotations and statistics raised a page's visibility in generative engine answers, while keyword stuffing did worse than doing nothing (Aggarwal et al., GEO). The lesson for a small business is simple: say specific, checkable things.

Dijae's rule

If a customer would ask it, write the answer as a sentence on your website. Assistants can only repeat what they can read.

How do you know if assistants cite you?

Short answer: use the reports the platforms give you, then ask the assistants your customers' real questions once a month.

Google counts AI feature traffic inside the Search Console Performance report under the "Web" search type, so it is mixed with normal search traffic rather than shown separately. Bing Webmaster Tools goes further: its AI Performance report shows where your content is cited across Microsoft Copilot and Bing, and in June 2026 Microsoft previewed citation share, intents and topics (Bing Search Blog).

For ChatGPT and Perplexity, the practical test is manual. Keep a list of ten questions a buyer asks before choosing a business like yours, ask each assistant every month, and record whether you are named or linked.

What can you set up today?

  1. Open your robots.txt and confirm OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot and Bingbot are not blocked.
  2. Check in Google Search Console that your key pages are indexed.
  3. Write your hours, location, services and prices as plain text, one clear page per service.
  4. Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools and open the AI Performance report.
  5. Write down ten buyer questions and test them in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google and Copilot.

What this cannot promise

Doing all of this makes a business eligible. It does not guarantee a recommendation. No platform publishes its selection criteria, answers change between runs and between models, and Google notes that meeting its requirements does not guarantee crawling, indexing or serving. Think of it as removing the reasons to be skipped, then measuring what happens.

Frequently asked questions

How does ChatGPT decide which businesses to recommend?

OpenAI does not publish ranking criteria. Its documentation says ChatGPT decides when to search, uses search providers and its OAI-SearchBot crawler, and shows the pages it used as sources. A site that blocks OAI-SearchBot will not appear in ChatGPT search answers.

Do I need special schema or an AI text file?

Google says no for its AI Overviews and AI Mode: standard SEO applies, with no new AI files or special schema required. Clear, crawlable pages matter more.

How long until a robots.txt change takes effect?

OpenAI and Perplexity both say about 24 hours for their search crawlers. Google notes that recrawling can take days to months.

Can I see when AI assistants cite my site?

Bing Webmaster Tools reports citations across Copilot and Bing. Google mixes AI feature traffic into its Search Console Web report. For ChatGPT and Perplexity, test your key questions by hand each month.
