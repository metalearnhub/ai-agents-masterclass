GEO and SEO are not rivals. Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of getting your business cited inside AI answers, and most of it rests on the same foundation as search engine optimization (SEO): pages that can be crawled, indexed and trusted. What changes is what counts as a win, how you write the pages that matter most, and how you measure the result.

The 60-second brief

  1. Google says optimizing for its AI features "is optimizing for the search experience, and thus still SEO." Crawlable, indexed, helpful pages remain the entry ticket.
  2. The win moves from a click on a ranked link to a citation or mention inside an answer, so pages must be easy to quote and facts easy to verify.
  3. Google and Bing now report AI visibility for free. Measure it monthly instead of guessing.

Decision: keep your SEO basics, rewrite your ten most important pages answer-first with sourced facts, and add AI visibility to your monthly report.

Move 01 · Keep

What stays the same between SEO and GEO?

What

Crawling, indexing, helpful content and technical basics.

Why it matters

AI answers draw on pages the system can find and trust. If you are not in the pool, you cannot be cited.

Do this

Fix crawl and index problems before writing a single "AI-ready" page.

Google is direct about this. Its AI optimization guide, updated July 10, 2026, says that optimizing for generative AI search "is optimizing for the search experience, and thus still SEO." Its AI features page says there are "no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary." A page has to be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet; that is the whole entry requirement.

So the SEO checklist you already know still carries the weight: pages that load and render, no accidental blocks in robots.txt, clean internal links, one clear topic per page, accurate titles and descriptions, and content that a person would find satisfying. Structured data is not required for AI features, but Google still recommends it as part of normal SEO because it supports rich results.

For other assistants the same logic applies with their own crawlers. ChatGPT search and Perplexity read the web through their own bots, so a robots.txt rule that blocks them keeps you out of their answers. The full crawler table is in How AI Assistants Decide Which Businesses to Recommend.

Move 02 · Shift

What changes when customers ask AI instead of Google?

What

The unit of success, the shape of the question and the way pages are read.

Why it matters

An answer cites a few sources. Being quotable decides whether one of them is you.

Do this

Rewrite key pages answer-first, with dated facts and named sources.

Four shifts matter for a business owner:

ShiftClassic searchAI answersWhat to change
What counts as a winA high position and a clickBeing cited or named inside the answerTrack citations and mentions, not only rankings
The questionA few keywordsA full question with contextWrite H2s as the questions customers actually ask
How pages are foundOne query, one results pageSeveral related searches per question (Google calls it query fan-out)Cover the subtopics around your main service, one clear page each
What earns trustLinks and relevanceClear, verifiable facts and consistent mentions across the webAdd sources and dates; keep listings and profiles consistent

The research backs the writing change. The 2024 GEO study from Princeton and partners, accepted at KDD 2024, tested ways of rewriting web pages and found the best methods raised a source's visibility in AI answers by up to 40%. Adding quotations, statistics and cited sources helped; keyword stuffing did worse than doing nothing. The detail of those methods is in What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

In practice, answer-first means the first two or three sentences of a page answer its title question and still make sense if an assistant quotes them alone. Facts carry a number, a date and a link. Each page owns one topic, so an assistant running several related searches finds a clean page for each subtopic instead of one page that half-answers everything.

Move 03 · Filter

What can you ignore in GEO advice?

What

Special files, AI markup and content chopped up "for the bots".

Why it matters

Effort spent on tricks is effort taken from the pages customers read.

Do this

Check any GEO tactic against the platform's own documentation before you pay for it.

A lot of GEO advice sells work that the platforms say they do not need. Google's guide lists several things you can skip for its Search features:

  • New machine-readable files: "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, markup, or Markdown to appear in Google Search." Google Search does not use llms.txt; it will not help or hurt your visibility there, though other services may read it.
  • Chunking: "There's no requirement to break your content into tiny pieces for AI to better understand it." There is no ideal page length; write for people.
  • Volume plays: producing many pages mainly to influence AI answers falls under Google's scaled content abuse policy.
  • Ranking promises: Google warns that no third-party tool has access to its internal ranking or AI systems.

For reference, check Google's AI optimization guide here and its AI features page here.

Move 04 · Measure

How do you measure visibility in AI search?

What

Two free reports plus a fixed set of customer questions you test by hand.

Why it matters

Rankings alone no longer show whether customers meet you inside answers.

Do this

Verify both consoles and log ten questions every month.

  • Google Search Console, Generative AI performance report: impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode over time, by page, country and device. Google says it rolled out to all websites worldwide as of August 31, 2026. AI feature traffic is also counted inside the normal Performance report under the Web search type.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools, AI Performance: where your content is cited in AI answers across Microsoft Copilot, Bing and select partners, and the grounding queries behind those citations. Public preview since February 2026; citation share, intents, topics and compare were added in preview on June 16, 2026.
  • A manual prompt set: ten real customer questions asked each month in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and Copilot. Log whether you are named, which page is cited and which competitors appear.

For reference, check the Search Console report documentation here and Bing's announcement here.

An honest limitation: neither report shows ChatGPT or Perplexity, the manual prompt test is a small sample that changes from day to day, and AI answers vary by user and wording. Treat the numbers as direction over months, not a score for a single week.

Dijae's rule

Earn the click first, then earn the quote. A page that is not good enough to rank is rarely good enough to be cited.

Frequently asked questions

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. Google says optimizing for its AI features is still SEO, and AI answers draw on pages that can be crawled and indexed. GEO adds a sharper focus on being quotable and measuring citations; it does not replace the basics.

Do I need an llms.txt file?

Not for Google. Google says its Search does not use llms.txt and that no new AI text files or markup are needed to appear in Google Search. Some other services may read it, so it does no harm, but it is not a priority.

How do I know if AI search is sending me traffic?

Use the Generative AI performance report in Google Search Console for AI Overviews and AI Mode, and the AI Performance report in Bing Webmaster Tools for Copilot citations. For ChatGPT and Perplexity, test a fixed set of customer questions by hand each month.

Does structured data help with AI search?

It is not required for Google's AI features, but Google still recommends it as part of normal SEO because it supports rich results. Use it for accuracy, not as a shortcut into AI answers.
