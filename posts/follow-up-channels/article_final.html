The 60-second brief

  1. No single channel wins. Each one has its own rule for when you are allowed to message, its own consent requirement and its own cost model.
  2. Messenger and WhatsApp are strongest inside the 24 hours after the customer last wrote. Email and SMS reach people later, but only with consent and proper sender setup.
  3. The best follow-up starts on the channel the customer chose, then moves to an owned channel, with permission, for the longer sequence.

Decision: reply where the conversation started, ask for permission to continue elsewhere, and match each message type to the channel built for it.

Move 01 · Reply

Start where the customer started

What

Answer on the channel the customer used, while their window is open.

Why it matters

That is where they are waiting, and where your reply costs least.

Do this

Make the first reply instant and useful, then ask how they prefer to hear from you next.

A customer who messages your Facebook Page is already in Messenger. Moving them to email in the first reply adds friction for no gain. On Messenger, a business can message freely for 24 hours after the customer's last message, and up to 7 days when the conversation started from a Click-to-Messenger ad. WhatsApp works the same way: a 24-hour customer service window opens each time the customer writes. Inside those windows, follow-up is easy. After them, the rules tighten. The speed side is in lead response time.

Move 02 · Rules

Know each channel's rules before you automate

What

Each channel decides when you may message, with what consent, at what cost.

Why it matters

Breaking a rule gets messages blocked, filtered as spam or accounts restricted.

Do this

Write the rule for each channel into your automation before the first sequence goes live.

Move 04 · Sequence

Build a cross-channel sequence, with permission

What

A follow-up plan that starts in chat and continues on an owned channel.

Why it matters

Chat windows close; a buyer who needs a week to decide still needs your follow-up.

Do this

Ask for email or phone consent inside the window, with a clear reason.

Keep the consent request honest and specific: “Can I email you the price list and check in on Thursday?” works better than a generic form. In the Philippines, record that consent; the steps are in the Data Privacy Act guide for chatbots. The follow-up messages themselves are in how to automate lead follow-up.

Dijae's rule

Follow up where the conversation lives. Move it to another channel only with the customer's permission and a reason they would agree with.

Move 05 · Measure

Measure each channel on what it costs to move a lead

What

Three numbers per channel, reviewed monthly.

Why it matters

A cheap channel that nobody reads is the most expensive one.

Do this

Tag every follow-up with its channel so the numbers separate cleanly.

Success metric: cost per booked call (or per order) by channel, next to reply rate and opt-out or block rate. Keep a channel when it books at a cost you accept and its opt-outs stay flat.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best channel for customer follow-up?

The one the customer used first, for the first replies. For longer follow-up, email works for most businesses because it allows messages at any time with consent; SMS and WhatsApp suit short, time-critical reminders.

Can I follow up on Messenger after 24 hours?

Only in limited ways: approved utility messages for transactional updates, the human-agent tag for a person replying within 7 days, or paid marketing and sponsored messages. Promotional follow-up outside the window needs opt-in or ads.

Is WhatsApp follow-up free?

Not entirely. WhatsApp charges per message by category since July 1, 2025. From October 1, 2026, each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, and utility templates inside the 24-hour window are charged.

Do I need to register to send business SMS in the US?

Yes. Application-to-person texts from standard 10-digit numbers must be registered for 10DLC, and unregistered traffic has been blocked since February 1, 2025.
