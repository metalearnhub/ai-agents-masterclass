Chatrace and ChatbotBuilder both promise the same thing: one AI chatbot that answers customers on Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and more, from one dashboard, for a small monthly fee. On their pricing pages they look almost identical. They are not, and the differences decide whether the tool fits the way your business actually sells.

This comparison is for small business owners, consultants and agencies choosing a multichannel chatbot platform. You will get a side-by-side of plans, channels and AI, the hidden cost most comparisons skip, a simple way to choose, and a trial test plan so you decide on evidence instead of a sales page.

The short answer

  • Chatrace fits businesses that sell on Meta channels plus TikTok or Viber and want comment automation, a CRM and sales pipelines in one account. Pro is $39 per month per account, up to 3,000 contacts.
  • ChatbotBuilder fits businesses that want SMS, email and voice in the same plan and a bundled AI allowance. Pro is $49 per month with an OpenAI key that includes 5,000,000 tokens.
  • Neither price is your full cost. WhatsApp messages are billed by Meta on top, and the rules changed on October 1, 2026.
  • Decide in the free trial, with your own conversations, using the test plan below.

2. Channels

Both platforms cover the channels most small businesses need: Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, voice and a chat widget for your website. The difference is at the edges.

  • Chatrace also lists TikTok and Viber, and it states that it is a Meta Business Partner and an official WhatsApp Business API provider. If part of your audience messages you on TikTok or Viber, that alone can decide it.
  • ChatbotBuilder lists email as a conversation channel alongside SMS and voice in the Pro plan, which suits businesses that follow up across several channels from one bot.

So why does this matter? A channel you will not use is not a feature, it is noise. Count the channels where your customers already message you today, then check those first.

Dijae's rulePick the platform for the channel your customers already use and for the person who will run it every day, not for the longest feature list. A tool your team opens every morning beats a more powerful tool nobody understands.

3. AI and what it really costs

Both platforms let you train an AI agent on your business information so it answers questions, qualifies leads and guides buyers. They package the AI cost differently.

  • Chatrace says AI is included and no OpenAI account is required, and it lists several model families. You do not manage an API key.
  • ChatbotBuilder includes an OpenAI key with 5,000,000 tokens on Pro, and lets you bring your own AI. On the White Label Enterprise plan you must connect your own API key.

4. The cost both pricing pages leave out

If WhatsApp matters to you, the platform fee is not the whole bill. Meta charges for WhatsApp Business messages on a per-message basis, by message category and country, since July 1, 2025.

The rule that changed recently: from October 1, 2026, Meta resumed charging for service messages, the replies you send inside a customer service window, which had been free since November 2024. Each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages per month, and charges start with the 1,001st. Messages from customers to you stay free. For reference, check the documentation here.

Watch outAn AI agent that answers every WhatsApp message can pass 1,000 service messages a month quickly. Budget for Meta's message charges separately from the $39 or $49 platform fee, and add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager, or replies beyond the free tier will not be delivered.

5. For agencies: white label

Both platforms sell a white-label version so agencies can offer the platform under their own brand, domain and prices.

  • Chatrace White Label is $499 per month with 50 client accounts and 500,000 contacts. Chatrace also lists agency tools: installing your flows in client accounts as templates, limiting what clients can see, and keeping ownership of bots clients create through your agency link.
  • ChatbotBuilder White Label Enterprise is $2,499 per month with unlimited accounts and 1M contacts included. You connect your own AI key, and white-label sales are final and not refundable.

For most small agencies, the deciding question is not price but support load. Every client you resell becomes your support ticket. Price your service with that in mind, using the tiers in how to package and price AI automation services.

7. Run the trial like a test

Chatrace gives you 7 days and ChatbotBuilder gives you 14. That is enough time to decide, but only if you plan the test before you start. Run both with the same inputs.

1Collect 30 real customer questions from your inbox, including five difficult ones: a complaint, a price objection, a question your FAQ does not answer, a request for a person, and an order change.

2Load the same business information into both platforms: products, prices, policies and the handoff rule.

3Connect your main channel and send every question from a test account, exactly as a customer would.

4Score every answer as correct, partly correct or wrong, and note every time the bot should have handed off and did not.

5Let the person who will run it daily build one small change on their own, such as a new answer or a tag, and time it.

Acceptance criteria before you pay:

  • At least 27 of the 30 answers are correct, and none of the wrong answers is about price or policy.
  • Every request for a person reaches a person, with the conversation history attached.
  • New leads land in your CRM or sheet with name, need and source filled in.
  • Your daily operator can make a simple change without help.
  • You know the monthly cost at your volume, including AI usage and WhatsApp message charges.

An honest limitation: this comparison is based on what each vendor publishes, and a feature on a pricing page tells you nothing about how deep, stable or easy it is. Two platforms can both list "WhatsApp" and behave very differently once real customers arrive. Only your own trial, with your own conversations, settles that.

Frequently asked questions

Is Chatrace or ChatbotBuilder cheaper?

On list price, Chatrace Pro is $39 per month per account with up to 3,000 contacts, and ChatbotBuilder Pro is $49 per month. Your real cost also depends on contacts, AI usage and WhatsApp message charges from Meta.

Does Chatrace support TikTok?

Yes. Chatrace lists TikTok and Viber among its channels, along with Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram and SMS. ChatbotBuilder does not list TikTok or Viber on its pricing or home page.

Do I need my own OpenAI account?

Not on either main plan. Chatrace includes AI without an OpenAI account, and ChatbotBuilder Pro includes an OpenAI key with 5,000,000 tokens. ChatbotBuilder's White Label Enterprise plan requires your own key.

Which free trial is longer?

ChatbotBuilder offers a 14-day sandbox with no credit card and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Chatrace offers a 7-day free trial with no credit card.
