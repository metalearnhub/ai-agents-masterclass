Move 01 · Understand

Know what speed is worth

What

Two widely cited studies measured what happens as response time grows.

Why it matters

Interest is highest the moment someone reaches out, and it fades fast.

Do this

Treat response time as a revenue number, not a service nicety.

The Lead Response Management Study, presented in 2007 by Dr. James Oldroyd of MIT's Sloan School and InsideSales.com, analyzed more than 15,000 leads and over 100,000 call attempts across six companies. The odds of contacting a lead were 100 times lower at 30 minutes than at 5 minutes, and the odds of qualifying it were 21 times lower.

Four years later, the same researchers published "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" in Harvard Business Review. They sent web leads to 2,241 US companies. Only 37% responded within an hour, the average response time was 42 hours, and 23% never responded at all. Companies that reached out within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify the lead as those that waited even an hour longer, and more than 60 times as likely as those that waited 24 hours or more.

Move 02 · Diagnose

Measure your real response time

What

Time from the moment a lead arrives to the first reply, and to the first human contact.

Why it matters

Most teams believe they are faster than they are.

Do this

Send yourself a test lead on every channel and time the replies.

The gap is not an old problem. In a 2026 test, Workato requested demos from 114 B2B companies. The average personalized email reply took 11 hours and 54 minutes, the average phone follow-up took 14 hours and 29 minutes, only 1 company sent a personal email within 5 minutes, and nearly 1 in 5 never replied by email at all.

Run the same test on your own business this week. Send a lead through your website form, your Facebook Page, your ads and your phone line, and write down two times for each: the first reply, and the first time a person actually engages. Those two numbers are your baseline.

Move 03 · Automate

Make the first reply instant and useful

What

An automatic first reply on every channel, in seconds.

Why it matters

It catches the lead while they are still looking, even when your team is busy.

Do this

Answer the likely question and offer the next step in the same message.

An instant reply only helps if it moves the conversation forward. "Thanks, we will get back to you" is fast but empty. A useful first reply confirms what the lead asked for, answers the most common question, and offers one clear next step: a booking link, a price list, or two short qualifying questions. On Messenger and Instagram, an AI agent or chatbot does this well, using platforms such as Botcake or ManyChat (affiliate links). The writing side is covered in chatbot conversation design, and the leaks that slow Messenger replies are in why businesses lose customers in Messenger.

Move 04 · Route

Route hot leads to a person, fast

What

A rule that alerts a named person the moment a lead shows buying intent.

Why it matters

Automation opens the conversation; people close it.

Do this

Define "hot", name an owner and set a time limit for human contact.

Routing is where most of the time is lost. In the 2026 Workato test, companies using a lead routing tool responded in an average of 3 hours and 32 minutes, against nearly 13 hours for those without one. Routing does not need to be complicated: tag the lead as hot when it asks about price, availability or a booking, assign it to one person, and alert that person in the tool they already watch.

The handoff itself, including what the bot says while a person takes over, is in the handoff playbook.

Dijae's rule

The first reply is yours to lose. Make it instant, make it useful, and make sure one person owns what happens next.

Move 05 · Prove

Cover the off-hours, then prove it with numbers

What

A plan for nights, weekends and lunch breaks, and a weekly speed report.

Why it matters

Leads do not arrive in office hours, and slow replies hide in averages.

Do this

Track the median and the slowest 10% of replies, not just the average.

After hours, the automatic reply carries the conversation: it answers, books where it can and tells the lead exactly when a person will be back. For calls, an AI voice receptionist does the same job on the phone line. Then follow up on a schedule; the sequence is in how to automate lead follow-up.

Success metric: median time from lead arrival to the first human contact for qualified leads, tracked weekly by channel, with the slowest 10% of replies reviewed by name.

Speed-to-lead checklist
Every channel sends a useful automatic reply within seconds
"Hot lead" is defined in writing (price, availability, booking)
Every hot lead has a named owner and an alert
Human contact target: within 1 hour in business hours
Off-hours reply states when a person will be back
Test leads are sent monthly on every channel and timed

An honest limitation: the best-known studies are from 2007 and 2011, they measured US web leads, and they show strong patterns rather than proof that speed alone causes the sale. A fast reply cannot rescue a weak offer or the wrong lead. Treat the research as a direction, then test it against your own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good lead response time?

Aim for an automatic, useful reply within seconds and human contact with qualified leads within five minutes to one hour. Research found that the odds of contacting and qualifying a lead fall sharply after the first five minutes.

What does speed to lead mean?

Speed to lead is the time between a lead reaching out (a form, a message, a call) and your business responding. It is usually measured as time to first reply and time to first human contact.

How fast do most businesses respond to leads?

Slowly. A 2011 study of 2,241 US companies found an average response time of 42 hours, and a 2026 test of 114 B2B companies found an average email reply time of almost 12 hours.

Can AI reply to leads instantly?

Yes. An AI chatbot or voice agent can reply in seconds on chat channels and the phone, answer common questions and book appointments. Keep a person responsible for qualified leads, and route them quickly.
